01 · Current Portfolio Project

Environmental Operational BI Warehouse

A dimensional warehouse integrating operational sources to analyze project-level revenue, cost, and profitability.

Skills: SQL, MySQL, Python, dimensional modeling, ETL design, data validation, Tableau.

What changed during the build: early testing showed that management labor and fixed/other project costs were not fully represented in the first profitability view. The model is being refined to represent the business more faithfully.
