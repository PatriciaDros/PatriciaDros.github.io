About Patricia

Curious by nature. Analytical by habit.

I'm Patricia — a data and operations analyst who likes figuring out how things work, where they break, and how to make the information more useful.

My path into analytics wasn't a straight line. I spent years in education, then moved into environmental consulting operations. When I entered the industry, I had no environmental consulting background and there wasn't a single system that showed me how all the pieces fit together.

So I started building small solutions for the problems in front of me. A completion checklist became project tracking. Project tracking became payroll and laboratory systems. Those systems exposed relationships between projects, contracts, billing, and costs. Eventually, the work became a dimensional data warehouse.

What I enjoy most: taking a messy process, understanding the business behind it, and turning it into something structured enough to trust.
