About Patricia

Curious by nature. Analytical by habit.

I'm Patricia — a data and operations analyst who likes figuring out how things work, where they break, and how to make the information more useful.

My background is in education and operations. Over time, that work led me into data analytics and business intelligence, where I found I especially enjoy messy, real-world data and the challenge of turning scattered information into something people can actually use.

These days, that work has grown into data modeling, SQL, Python, business intelligence, and a dimensional data warehouse built from real operational data.

What I enjoy most: taking a messy process, understanding the business behind the numbers, and turning it into something structured enough to trust.
